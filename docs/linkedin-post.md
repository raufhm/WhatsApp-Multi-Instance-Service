Weekend project: I made a short recorded preview for Whops, a self-hosted shared WhatsApp inbox for small teams.

The idea is simple: many small businesses already have the WhatsApp number their customers know. Whops explores what happens when that number becomes a shared team workspace instead of living on one person's phone.

This preview shows the current product direction:

- a shared inbox for customer conversations
- channel health and queue monitoring
- assignment and handoff workflows
- TOTP and backup-code security settings
- a setup flow for a small business workspace

I also made a public-safe version of the demo data. All phone numbers, account IDs, and emails are masked or replaced with fictional examples before sharing.

Tech stack: Go, React, PostgreSQL, and whatsmeow.

Still early, still experimental, but it has been a useful way to learn the real operational details behind customer messaging: who owns the reply, what needs follow-up, when a queue is healthy, and where context tends to get lost.
